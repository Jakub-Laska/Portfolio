Professional Experience

Buyer Assistant (Sponsoring Department)

Right now I'm working in the sponsoring department at OTCF S.A., the company behind the 4F brand. On paper it's "assistant," but in reality it's more like doing two jobs at once: handling all the typical assistant and admin stuff while independently leading full production cycles – from initial negotiations to final delivery and quality checks.

My day-to-day is a mix of detective work and juggling chaos: debugging what went wrong in a supply chain (delays, quality issues, logistics nightmares), negotiating impossible deadlines with suppliers, and coordinating massive productions under super tight timelines. I manage multiple clothing and accessory lines at the same time, working with suppliers across Poland, Europe, and Asia – that means constant communication in different time zones, languages, and cultures.

I handle everything from data analysis in Excel and our ERP system, purchase orders, invoice settlements, on-site factory inspections, to cross-department collaboration with design, tech, logistics, and finance teams. I was even selected as a beta tester for our internal ERP – constantly giving feedback to make it more user-friendly and efficient (turns out people say I’m pretty good at spotting system issues and suggesting fixes).
