About Me

I’ve always been drawn to computers – as a kid and teenager I spent countless hours gaming, tweaking setups, and messing around with tech. When I eventually outgrew gaming, I felt a bit lost. Nothing really grabbed me the same way… until I discovered coding.

The moment I wrote my first lines of HTML and CSS and saw something I built appear in the browser, I was hooked. That feeling of turning an idea in my head into something real and interactive – it’s the closest thing I’ve found to the flow I used to get from games, but way more rewarding.

My background isn’t typical for a developer. I graduated with a bachelor’s in Business Linguistics, and right now I work as a buyer assistant at 4F in the fashion industry. Before that, I was a customer advisor. Pretty much my entire professional life has involved working with people – clients, suppliers (Polish and international), teams, negotiations, problem-solving on the fly. It taught me how to communicate clearly, stay calm under pressure, listen well, and collaborate effectively.

Outside of work and coding, I stay active – I used to train volleyball competitively, and I still play sports regularly. I also love cooking (nothing beats experimenting in the kitchen after a long day of coding).

When it comes to front-end, I’m self-taught and proud of it. I learn fast, write clean and readable code, and care about the details – whether it’s pixel-perfect layout, accessibility, or smooth user experience.

I’m looking for a junior front-end position where I can keep growing, contribute from day one, and work alongside people who are just as excited about building great things for the web as I am.
